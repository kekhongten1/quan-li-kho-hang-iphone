UNG DUNG JAVA SWING QUAN LI KHO HANG IPHONE

1. Cai dat CSDL
- Mo MySQL va chay file sql/tao_co_so_du_lieu.sql
- Mac dinh app ket noi:
  + host: localhost
  + port: 3306
  + database: quan_li_kho_iphone
  + user: root
  + password: 123456

Neu can doi cau hinh, dat bien moi truong truoc khi chay:
- QLKH_DB_HOST
- QLKH_DB_PORT
- QLKH_DB_NAME
- QLKH_DB_USER
- QLKH_DB_PASSWORD

2. Tai khoan mac dinh
- admin / 123
- quanly / 123
- nhanvien / 123

3. Chay chuong trinh
- Windows: chay chay_windows.bat
- Linux/macOS: chay ./chay_linux_mac.sh

4. Chuc nang da co
- Dang nhap tu MySQL
- Phan quyen: quan ly va nhan vien (tai khoan admin van co day du quyen quan ly)
- Quan li san pham: quan ly duoc them, sua, xoa; nhan vien chi duoc xem va tim kiem
- Trang thai san pham tu dong theo ton kho: Dang kinh doanh / Sap het hang / Het hang; Tam ngung do admin cap nhat thu cong
- Nhap kho: khoa gia nhap theo gia luu trong san pham, phieu nhan vien tao se cho duyet, quan ly co the duyet hoac huy kem ghi chu
- Xuat kho: khoa gia ban, khong cho xuat am ton, khong cho xuat san pham tam ngung hoac het hang, phieu nhan vien tao se cho duyet
- Thong ke bao cao: ton kho, san pham sap het - het hang, san pham tam ngung, phieu da duyet gan day, phieu da huy gan day
